SECTION 02 / THE LOGO
09

The Primary Mark

The emblem carries the brand story: two vessels on one heading, a compass for judgment, a rising route for growth, and a dragon for tenacity — all held inside a single rope ring.

Always reproduce from the master artwork files. Never redraw, trace, or rebuild the mark.
The emblem works as a seal: app icons, watermarks, document headers, and merchandise.
In running layouts, pair it with the typographic name set in the headline typeface.

SECTION 02 / THE LOGO
11

Clearspace & Minimum Size

X X X X

The exclusion zone X equals one quarter of the emblem's height, on all four sides. Nothing enters it — no type, imagery, or page edge.

48–96 PX / MICRO
220 PX

Use the simplified micro mark from 48–96 px on screen. Use the detailed emblem at 128 px or larger, or 25 mm in print. Use the typographic name when production cannot preserve the linework.

Proportion

COMMAND BLACK — 58%
CARBON — 20%
WHITE 12%
RED 7%

Signal Red occupies 5–10% of any page. It directs attention — it is never a background.

Red is reserved for primary actions, active states, section markers, and single signal moments. Deep Red supports it in gradients and hover states. Steel carries quiet information.

SECTION 04 / TYPOGRAPHY
21

Setting Rules

DO
Uppercase for short labels and major headlines only
Body copy in sentence case, 1.4–1.6 line height
Track uppercase mono labels at +10 to +14%
Left-align. Ragged right. Tight, deliberate hierarchy
DON'T
✕Stencil, distressed, camouflage, or novelty military fonts
✕Long passages set in all caps
✕Centered body paragraphs or justified text
✕More than two typefaces plus the mono on one surface
